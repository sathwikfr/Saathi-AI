## Verdict
Pass. It reads as one product: ivory paper, ocean blue, Bricolage + Figtree, warm honest copy, and the
same panel/button/notice components from landing to dashboard. The parent-facing notice page is the best
screen in the app (big type, 9 language chips, the 112 line in red). What holds it back is not taste,
it is a handful of mobile bugs and small inconsistencies, listed below.

## Findings

### P1 (fix before real families arrive)
1. **Profile page cut off on phones.** At 390px the tabs ("Delete account"), the hint text and the
   "Save changes" button run past the right edge and are clipped. Cause: `.account-layout` switches to
   `grid-template-columns: 1fr` at 880px; `1fr` won't shrink below the side-nav strip's width.
   Fix: `minmax(0, 1fr)` (globals.css ~4131). /impeccable adapt
2. **iPhone zooms into every form field.** `.form-input` is 0.96rem (15.4px); iOS Safari zooms any input
   under 16px on focus, then leaves the page zoomed. Every signup, login, onboarding and checkout field
   does this. Fix: 1rem on inputs/selects/textareas (globals.css ~697). /impeccable adapt
3. **Support address is a personal Gmail** (sath2901@gmail.com) on the FAQ card, Privacy, Terms, Refunds.
   For a paid health product this costs trust. Swap when the domain lands (it's env-driven). /impeccable clarify
4. **Bare 404 for a turned-off emergency card.** `/card/<old token>` shows Next's default white
   "404 This page could not be found" even in dark mode. The person opening it may be a neighbour or a
   hospital. Add `src/app/not-found.tsx` in the brand, and on the card route a message like "This card
   link was turned off by the family. In an emergency call 112." /impeccable harden

### P2
5. **Two different "primary" buttons.** Hero and featured plan use the violet/pink `.btn-glow`; the navbar,
   checkout, auth and dashboard use solid ocean blue. On the first screen both sit side by side.
   "Peace of mind for you" is also a blue-to-violet gradient (the detector flags 2 gradient-text spots).
   The brand note says violet was rejected; pick one primary. /impeccable colorize
6. **Prices formatted two ways.** Landing says ₹1,699; checkout, payment and the confirm step say ₹1699
   (`checkout/confirm/page.tsx:76,89,113`, `checkout/payment/page.tsx:316`). Use the
   `toLocaleString('en-IN')` helper the FAQ and emails already use. /impeccable polish
7. **Missing h1 on two screens.** Dashboard empty state ("Welcome, …", `dashboard/page.tsx:461`) and the
   invite error ("Invite not available", `invite/[token]/page.tsx:57`) are h3 with no h1. /impeccable audit
8. **Phone landing is very long (≈17 screens).** The comparison table becomes six stacked 2x2 blocks
   (~3 screens alone). On phones show only the Aaptha column with a "Compare" toggle, or 3 rows. /impeccable distill
9. **Small standalone links.** "Forgot password?" (110x22), "Create an account", "Log in",
   "Change plan" (80x17), "Back to login" are under the WCAG 2.2 24px target minimum and far from 44px.
   Pad them to 44px height. /impeccable adapt
10. **"7-day" breaks across lines** on the phone onboarding gate ("Start your 7- / day free trial first").
    Use a non-breaking hyphen (U+2011) in "7‑day". /impeccable polish
11. **Emails use Fraunces**, a font the site never uses (`lib/email.ts:148,179`). Use Bricolage or a
    system stack so the receipt looks like the site. /impeccable typeset

### P3
12. `transition: all` in 5 rules (globals.css 776, 987, 2811, 2978, 2999): name the properties.
13. Hover lifts on `.btn-primary`, `.btn-gold`, `.btn-ghost`, `.card-hover`, `.choice-card` are not behind
    `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`, so phones get a stuck lift after a tap (the landing ones
    were already gated on 2026-10-20).
14. `aria-label` on `<s>` strike prices is ignored by screen readers (axe "needs review" x5); put the old
    price in visually-hidden text instead.
15. `themeColor` is always ivory, so the phone's browser bar stays light in dark mode.
16. Bounce easings in `liveCallPhone.module.css:772,1053` and `safety.module.css:177` (film only; fine to keep).

## What is working well
- axe-core found **zero** WCAG A/AA violations on every route, both themes. Contrast holds in dark mode.
- No horizontal scrolling anywhere except the Profile clip above.
- One design system really is used: `.btn`, `.panel`, `.notice`, `.form-input` show up identically from
  checkout to dashboard. Dark mode is complete, not an afterthought.
- Copy is honest and specific ("Razorpay checks your card or bank with about ₹5 and refunds it",
  "Aaptha is not an emergency service. In an emergency call 112").
- Checkout is clear: stepper, "Due today ₹0", first-charge date, consent checkboxes.
- Reduced-motion handling exists in 20 files; `.btn:active` press feedback exists.

## Landing speed (2026-10-22, uncommitted)
Measured on a local production build, emulated phone (390px), main-thread CPU from Chrome traces (wall-clock
numbers on this machine were too noisy). The live site took ~13 s to first paint on a 4x-slowed phone.
- Root cause: laying out the whole ~1,600-element page during load and on every animation frame.
  Fix: `.landing > .section { content-visibility: auto }` (globals.css). A/B on the same build: 10.9 -> 5.8 ms
  main-thread work per frame, 515 -> 838 frames drawn in the same 6 s. Nav # links still land exactly
  (SmoothScroll lays everything out before a glide; checked #plans/#faq/#why/#safety on desktop).
- Navbar colour hit test runs once per frame (was forcing full layouts in hydration and on every scroll event).
- Stagger lists: CSS transitions + IntersectionObserver instead of GSAP from() tweens (style read/write thrash).
- Lenis smooth scroll off on touch screens (its per-frame tick kept phones drawing frames while idle).
- Navbar "live" dot pulses with scale/opacity instead of box-shadow.
- Tried and dropped: a rotating glow layer (no gain), a masked rotating pricing border (13 MB GPU layer).
Not done: the hero orb + waveform still draw every frame while the hero is on screen (by design).
